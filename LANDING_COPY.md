## FAQ

**Q: Is this really free?**
A: Yes. StackSavings is completely free to use. No credit card, no login, no catch. It's a free tool built by Credex to help startups understand their AI spend.

**Q: How accurate are the savings estimates?**
A: Every number traces back to official vendor pricing pages, verified weekly. The audit engine uses the same logic a finance person would use — comparing what you pay against what the plan should cost for your team size. Sources are documented in our pricing data.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: We store the audit result (tool names and spend amounts) to power the shareable URL feature. We do not store personally identifiable information unless you choose to enter your email for the report. We never sell your data.

**Q: What is Credex?**
A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, and others — sourced from companies that overforecast or pivoted. For teams with significant AI spend, Credex credits can save 20-40% on top of the audit recommendations.

**Q: What if my tools aren't listed?**
A: We currently support the 8 most common AI tools for startup engineering teams: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. More tools will be added based on user requests.
