# Go-To-Market Strategy — StackSavings

## Exact Target User

Not "startups" — that's too broad. The exact user is:

**Engineering Manager or CTO at a seed-to-Series A startup (10-50 people) who owns the engineering budget and notices the AI tools line item growing every month but has no benchmark to know if they're overspending.**

Specifically:
- Title: Engineering Manager, VP Engineering, CTO, or Technical Co-founder
- Company stage: Post-product, 10-50 employees, $500k-$5M ARR
- Pain: Pays for Cursor, GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT Team, and Claude simultaneously without knowing if all are necessary
- Trigger moment: Monthly budget review, new hire onboarding (adding seats), or a finance person asking "why is this line item so high?"

## How to Get First 100 Users in 30 Days with $0

**Week 1 — Seed with authenticity:**
Post on Hacker News: "Show HN: I built a free tool that audits your AI tool spend — found $400/mo in waste for my own team." Real numbers, real story. HN readers are exactly the target user. One good Show HN can drive 500-2000 visitors.

**Week 2 — Reddit distribution:**
Post in r/ExperiencedDevs and r/startups with a specific framing: "We were paying for Cursor Business, GitHub Copilot Enterprise, and ChatGPT Team simultaneously for a 4-person team. Built a tool to audit this. Here's what we found." Specific and honest performs 10x better than generic.

**Week 3 — Twitter/X thread:**
Write a thread: "I audited our AI tool spend and found we were wasting $380/mo. Here's a breakdown of every tool, what we cut, and what we kept." End with the tool link. Tag relevant accounts. Threads with real numbers get shared.

**Week 4 — Direct outreach:**
DM 20 engineering managers on LinkedIn or Twitter who have posted about AI tools. Not "check out my tool" — send them their audit result directly: "I ran a quick audit on what a typical team your size pays for [tools they mentioned] — found potential savings. Made a free tool for this."
