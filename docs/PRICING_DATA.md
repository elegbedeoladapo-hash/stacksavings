## Notes

- API-direct pricing (Anthropic API, OpenAI API, ChatGPT API direct, Gemini API) is usage-based and varies by model and token count. The audit engine flags these as "pay as you go" and does not apply seat-based savings logic.
- Enterprise pricing for Cursor, Claude, and ChatGPT requires contacting sales and varies by contract.
- Prices are in USD per user per month on monthly billing. Annual billing typically offers 15-20% discount — not factored into audit engine to keep comparisons conservative.
- Gemini Ultra price based on Google One AI Premium plan as of verification date.
