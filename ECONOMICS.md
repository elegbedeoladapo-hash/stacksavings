# Economics — StackSavings

## What a Converted Lead is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. Based on publicly available information:

- Average AI tool spend for a 10-50 person startup: $500-$2,000/month
- Credex discount vs retail: ~20-40%
- If a startup moves $1,000/month of AI spend through Credex credits, Credex earns the margin on that spend
- Estimated margin per customer: $200-$400/month = $2,400-$4,800/year

**Conservative estimate: One converted customer = $3,000 ARR to Credex**

**Blended CAC at early stage: ~$10-20 per audit completed**
**CAC per paying Credex customer: $50-200** (assuming 10-20% conversion from audit to consultation, 30-50% consultation to purchase)

## Conversion Funnel Math
## Conversion Funnel Math
1,000 visitors to StackSavings
→ 200 audits completed (20% conversion — motivated visitors)
→ 60 emails captured (30% of auditors)
→ 20 consultation requests (10% of auditors, skewed to high-savings cases)
→ 8 Credex credit purchases (40% close rate on consultations)
→ $24,000 ARR added (8 customers × $3,000 avg ARR)

At this funnel, **every 1,000 visitors = ~$24,000 ARR for Credex.**

Cost to acquire 1,000 visitors via organic channels: ~$0-50 in time.
Cost via paid: ~$500-1,500.

Organic CAC payback: immediate.
Paid CAC payback: 1-2 months.

## What Would Have to Be True for $1M ARR in 18 Months

**The math:**
- $1M ARR ÷ $3,000 avg customer ARR = 333 paying Credex customers
- 333 customers ÷ 40% close rate = 833 consultations booked
- 833 consultations ÷ 10% audit-to-consultation rate = 8,330 audits completed
- 8,330 audits ÷ 20% visitor-to-audit rate = 41,650 total visitors needed

**41,650 visitors over 18 months = ~2,300 visitors/month**

This is very achievable. A single successful Hacker News post drives 500-2,000 visitors. Two or three viral distribution moments plus steady SEO gets to 2,300/month within 6 months.

**What must be true:**
1. The audit engine produces savings recommendations that feel accurate and defensible — users trust the numbers
2. The Credex consultation converts at 40%+ — the offer must be compelling and the savings real
3. Average customer retention is 12+ months — AI tool spend is sticky, customers don't churn easily
4. Referral loop works — users share their audit links, driving organic traffic

## Key Assumptions and Risks

**Assumptions:**
- $3,000 avg ARR per customer is conservative — large teams could be worth $10k+/year
- 20% visitor-to-audit conversion assumes motivated, relevant traffic (HN, Reddit)
- 40% consultation close rate assumes Credex has a strong sales motion

**Risks:**
- AI tool vendors change pricing frequently — audit engine needs regular updates
- If ChatGPT or Cursor drops prices significantly, savings opportunities shrink
- Enterprise customers (highest value) may require procurement process that slows conversion
